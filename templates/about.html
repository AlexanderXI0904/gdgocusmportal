About This Portal

GDGoC USM Internal Portal

The GDGoC USM Portal is a centralized workspace designed to host custom internal tools that streamline committee operations and automate repetitive technical workflows.

Currently, the portal features our flagship Mass Email Application—a robust tool that allows committee members to prepare and dispatch personalised bulk emails seamlessly using Excel, HTML, and Microsoft Graph.

Current Capabilities

  • Personalised emails — Automatically inserts information from your Excel data into each email.
  • Email editor — Create and customise messages using either a visual editor or HTML.
  • Campaign management — Save and load email templates for reuse.
  • Bulk dispatch — Process large batches of emails in the background while keeping track of sending progress.
  • Microsoft integration — Uses Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft Graph for secure account authentication and email delivery.

Built for GDGoC USM

This architecture was developed specifically to support the communication and operational needs of Google Developer Group On Campus, Universiti Sains Malaysia (GDGoC USM), providing the committee with a scalable foundation for future internal applications.

Internal Use

This application is strictly intended for authorised GDGoC USM committee use.
